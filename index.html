N° 05 — How it works

Inside a fountain pen

  1. 1

    The ink waits

    Ink sits in a small tank called a converter or a cartridge.

  2. 2

    The feed breathes

    Tiny channels let ink flow down while air goes up.

  3. 3

    The nib writes

    A thin slit pulls ink to the tip and onto your paper.

N° 08 — FAQ

Questions

Is a fountain pen hard to use?

Not at all. Hold it at a gentle angle, do not press hard, and let the ink do the work.

Can left-handed people use fountain pens?

Yes. Choose a fine nib and a quick drying ink to avoid smudges.

Do you engrave names on pens?

Yes, engraving is free on every fountain pen and takes one extra day.

How long does an ink bottle last?

A 50 ml bottle lasts most people around six months of daily writing.
